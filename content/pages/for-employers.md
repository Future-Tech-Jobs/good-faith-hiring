## How to adopt it

1. **Check your adverts.** Run each new advert through the checklist below. Commitments 1 to 5 are about the advert itself.
2. **Plan your replies.** Set up an acknowledgement that goes out within 5 working days and says how to ask for adjustments. Decide who sends outcomes and feedback, and when.
3. **Say you follow it.** Add a line and a link to your adverts. The [adopt page](/adopt/) has text to copy.
4. **Get listed, if you like.** Send us three live adverts and we'll add you to the [adopters list](/adopters/), which also lets you use the badge.

Following it is free, and so is being listed.

## Common sticking points

**"We can't show pay because it depends on the person."** Then show the range you'd pay. If it's wider than 1.5×, you're probably hiring for two levels, so advertise two roles.

**"Our hiring managers want long requirement lists."** Ask them which five things they'd turn someone down for. Those are the must-haves. Everything else is a nice-to-have.

**"We get too many applications to reply to all of them."** An automatic acknowledgement and a batch outcome message take minutes to set up. Clear adverts also mean fewer applications from people who can't take the job.
